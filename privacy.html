Privacy

Your privacy matters. This page explains our plain-language commitment to treating information shared through this website with care.

Respect for your information

Information you submit through this website is used to respond to your request, understand the service you need, and communicate about that request. We do not sell your personal information or share it with unrelated third parties for their marketing purposes.

Limited access and care

Information is shared only when reasonably necessary to provide, administer, or improve the requested service, or when required by law. We take practical steps to keep submitted information private and limit access to people who need it for the request.

Your choices

If you have questions about information you submitted or would like to request a correction, please contact Zasheira Li using the contact options on the homepage. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card numbers, or other highly sensitive information through general website forms.
