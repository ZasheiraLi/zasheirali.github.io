Return policy

Please review these terms before submitting a service request. They are written to make the cancellation and refund process clear from the beginning.

Cancellation window

Cancellation requests must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the original purchase or service request. After the 14-day period has ended, refunds are void and cannot be approved.

Refund conditions

  • Delivery, travel, processing, or other delivery fees are non-refundable.
  • If the requested service has already been completed, a full refund may not be applicable. Any review will consider the work delivered and the portion of the service already performed.
  • Refund requests may only be submitted by the main purchaser named on the original order.
  • The original receipt or order confirmation is required for every refund request.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the same payment method used for the purchase. We cannot redirect a refund to another person or payment method.

How to request a review

Contact Zasheira Li with your original receipt, purchaser name, order details, and a brief explanation of your request within the 14-day window. Submission of a request does not guarantee approval; eligibility is determined under the conditions above.
